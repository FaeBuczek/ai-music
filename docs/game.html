The Reveal

Actually… all three songs were AI generated.

And fun fact: the first song, “Walk My Walk” by Breaking Rust, hit #1 on Billboard's Country Digital Song Sales chart — an AI-generated track outperforming human country artists.

It's getting harder to tell the difference between AI-generated and human-made music. When algorithms start outperforming — and out-earning — real human artists, the ethical questions stack up fast. Who gets paid? Whose work trained the model? What happens to the artists whose careers were built on craft, time, and lived experience?

There aren't clean answers yet. But the fact that you may not have been able to tell is part of why those questions matter.
