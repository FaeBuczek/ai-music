Introduction

GenAI use in creative endeavors, such as music, poses a lot of interesting ethical questions such as copyright for music used in the training sets and for finished AI products, rights to financial compensation for AI generated music, as well as transparency about whether the production is AI influenced/generated. Similar to social media accounts that are entirely AI driven that trick some that stumble across them, there are artists that most listeners assume are creating their own music, but in reality are entirely AI driven.

Additionally, AI-powered algorithms are now fully deployed by big players in the music industry such as Billboard, Spotify, and Apple Music. AI driven algorithms heavily impact user behavior whether in music, arts, movies, or even social media.

We aim to explore the ways in which AI is being used to assist in the creation of / produce new music based on its training sets. We will analyze different aspects of AI usage such as AI generated music and AI artist personas.

We hope users can leave our website with new knowledge of all the aforementioned topics and learn about how this technology is advancing and what research has been done in terms of its implications technologically, societally, economically, and artistically. This is a new and developing topic in the world of music and human artistry with a lot of debate surrounding it. We hope users can leave our website having thought of options and solutions on how to move forward and address this topic.
