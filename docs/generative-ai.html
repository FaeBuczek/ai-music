Generative AI

Generative AI music can be produced and takes form in many different ways.

Vocals Generated

AI can synthesize singing voices from scratch or clone the voice of a real artist. These tools learn vocal timbre, phrasing, and emotion from training data, allowing producers to generate fully sung performances without a human in the booth.

Instruments Generated

AI models can produce individual instrument tracks such as guitar riffs, drum patterns, bass lines, or piano arrangements. These stems can be dropped into a project as if they were recorded by a session musician.

Remix Generated

Existing tracks can be reimagined by AI, shifting the genre, tempo, key, or mood. A pop song can be turned into a lo-fi rework, a hyperpop edit, or an orchestral arrangement with a single prompt.

Entire Song Generated

Platforms like Suno and Udio can generate a complete track, including vocals, instrumentation, structure, and mixing, from a short text prompt. The output is a finished song ready to stream.

Lyrics Generated

Large language models can write full sets of lyrics in any genre, theme, or style. They can mimic the voice of a specific artist, follow strict rhyme schemes, or generate hooks and verses on demand.
