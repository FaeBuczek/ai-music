How it is Made

Similar to text-based Generative AI, Gen AI Music is made with large databases of existing audio to train neural networks to identify patterns, rhythm, harmony, and instrumentation

They then use user prompts to generate music based off of the training material. Some popular AI music tools include: Suno, Tunee, Udio, and more.

Looking at the UI, the interface is quite similar to other Digital Audio Workstations.

Evolution of AI Music

Contrary to the status of generative AI music from early 2024 which were very hands on, the models have become more autonomous. As little as a few lines can generate an entire, complex piece of music.

Combining this with the increasing power of large language models, the auditory elements as well as the lyrics can all be generated automatically. Notably, these language models are also crucial to the interpretation user prompts using tools like Suno.
